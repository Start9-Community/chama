The problem

The people who need Bitcoin most
are handed custodial risk to get it.

Custodial exchanges

To turn sats into rent money, you route through an exchange that holds your funds, demands KYC, and can freeze, close, or vanish. "Not your keys" — at exactly the on/off-ramp where it hurts.

Local money is trust

Real trade happens between neighbors — M-Pesa, Wave, Orange Money, cash. That trust is social, but the tools are global platforms with no idea who your community is.

P2P without a referee

Pure peer-to-peer escrow needs someone to break ties. Hand that power to a company and you've rebuilt the custodian. Hand it to nobody and disputes strand funds.

Chama's bet: the community is the trust layer. The protocol just has to make sure no sat is ever stranded — and none is ever sent twice.

What Chama is

A coordinator, not a wallet.

Three open protocols, each doing one job. Chama is the thin, non-custodial layer that choreographs them — and holds nothing.

Identity · Coordination

Nostr

Your keypair is your account. Trades, chat, votes and ratings are signed Nostr events (kinds 38100–38108) — portable, replayable, no database, no login.

Value substrate

Fedimint

Client-side ecash (WASM) is the cryptographic material for the brief lock→claim window. Sats are split by Shamir Secret Sharing across the three roles — never a shared balance.

Universal interface

Lightning

BOLT11 in, BOLT11 out. Every sat enters and leaves through Lightning. Between trades your wallet sits at exactly zero.

"Chama does not custody funds. Every sat in the system is in transit through a specific trade."

— PHILOSOPHY.md §2.1

The escrow lifecycle

Funding is the lock.

The old JOIN / READY / LOCK ceremony collapsed into a single atomic side-effect of payment landing. Only two consent gates remain in the whole flow — and both move sats.

CREATED
Terms published to Nostr, awaiting payment.
LOCKED
Payment lands → ecash minted + SSS-split, atomically.
APPROVED
Gate 1 · VOTE — 2-of-3 agree on the outcome.
CLAIMED
Gate 2 · CLAIM — winner redeems the ecash.
COMPLETED
Paid out over Lightning. Wallet back to zero.
Never lose a sat

Fund-loss guards, cold-start reconciliation, crash-safe locks, a recovery banner. A stranded sat is a structural failure to repair — not an acceptable cost.

Never send one twice

When a payout's outcome is unknown — a watch timed out, a link dropped — Chama refuses to re-send and reconciles to the durable operation. Unknown ⇒ confirm, not retry.
