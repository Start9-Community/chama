# v5.4 plan: direct bond rollover and store continuity

Status: planned follow-up after v5.3. This document records the product and
engineering decisions from v5.3 field testing.

## Problem

When a commitment-bond term ends, the bond remains safely controlled by the
owner on-chain but no longer counts for arbiter selection or bonded-store
tenure. The current UI only offers reclaim destinations. Choosing Chama sends
the reclaim transaction to a Fedimint peg-in address, which can then require the
federation's configured on-chain finality delay before the ecash is spendable.
Making a fresh bond after that would require another withdrawal/funding cycle.

## Primary v5.4 flow

Add **Renew bond** beside **Reclaim**. Renewal directly spends the mature CLTV
bond UTXO into a newly constructed commitment-bond output with a new future
`lockUntil`:

`mature bond UTXO -> fresh bond output (+ optional owner-controlled change)`

This is one owner-signed on-chain transaction. It must not route through the
federation, convert to ecash, or require cabinet/co-signer custody.

The confirmation screen must show:

- renewed amount;
- selected term and exact estimated unlock date/block;
- miner fee and any owner-controlled change output;
- the fact that the old bond is already expired and the new term starts with
  the replacement bond;
- progress: signed/broadcast -> confirming -> active -> kind-38135 announcement
  published.

Publish the new bond announcement only after the replacement output satisfies
the bond funding confirmation policy. Preserve the old announcement/history as
expired; do not mutate history to make renewal appear retroactive.

## Existing-bond compatibility

Direct rollover should support bonds created before v5.4 when all of these are
true:

- the original bond UTXO is still unspent at its CLTV address;
- its lock height has passed;
- the active user seed can re-derive the persisted/announced owner key;
- the bond script, address, network, UTXO and ownership all recompute and verify.

Therefore an expired, unreclaimed v5.3 bond can be renewed after upgrading to
v5.4. This is compatibility with the old output, not retroactive eligibility:
the user remains unbonded between expiry and confirmation of the new output.

If the user has already reclaimed:

- to an external address: Chama cannot directly roll it over;
- into Fedimint: it becomes ecash after federation finality and a new bond must
  be funded through the normal funding path;
- to the Chama-derived bond-key address: a later enhancement may spend that
  owner-controlled return UTXO directly into a fresh bond, but this must be
  implemented and tested explicitly rather than assumed by the first rollover.

## Reclaim disclosure fix

Before **Back into Chama** is signed, query Fedimint on-chain info and show the
actual `finalityDelay`, minimum deposit, estimated miner fee and net deposit.
After broadcast, show confirmation progress against that exact delay. Do not
say merely "a few confirmations" and do not hardcode 10: the requirement is
federation-specific. Preserve the bond-key fallback and clearly report when it
was used.

## Store continuity

"Permanent store" means renewable continuity, never an irreversible permanent
lock. An active qualifying bond grants the existing bonded-store behavior:
rolling storefront renewal while the seller is online. v5.4 should connect
that entitlement to bond rollover:

- warn at approximately 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before bond expiry;
- offer renewal before and after expiry;
- keep the existing store/event identity where protocol-safe rather than
  requiring the seller to recreate inventory;
- provide a bounded confirmation grace state during a verified rollover so a
  storefront does not disappear solely because the replacement transaction is
  awaiting confirmation;
- never allow the grace state to grant arbiter selection or bonded privileges
  without a verifiable replacement transaction;
- immediately return to ordinary unbonded/manual-renew behavior when there is
  neither an active bond nor a valid pending rollover.

## Safety and tests

- Recompute every old and new Taproot script/address locally.
- Require the owner key and mature CLTV leaf; no alternate custody path.
- Verify every input against chain data and reject spent/mismatched UTXOs.
- Enforce a future minimum term and dust-safe outputs.
- Make broadcast idempotent and recover a lost local receipt from outspends.
- Test legacy v5.3 records, cross-device seed recovery, partial renewal/change,
  fee spikes, reorg/pending states, duplicate taps, and announcement failure.
- Never label a pending rollover active until its confirmation policy passes.
