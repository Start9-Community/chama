The wallet mental model is dead.

On Chama there's no balance sitting on a server, no app holding your money, nothing to freeze or hack. Sats appear only while a trade is live, then settle straight to you. You never lose one, you never send one twice.

That's not custody — it's coordination. Want to feel it? Pay someone's bill with a Community Bill Pay listing. Easiest first sat you'll ever earn.
