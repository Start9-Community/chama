# Chama — the "start your own fed" pull (the sovereignty ladder) — vision sketch

**Status:** vision / strategy sketch (2026-07-08). The *positive-pull* answer to "how do communities graduate from a
shared federation to their own?" — WITHOUT hobbling the shared experience. Post-v5 track; captured so the whole system
(bond, liveness, arbiters) can be built *toward* it.

## The one reframe that makes it click
Chama's promise is **local money**, and that has two layers:
- **Local trust** — your community's own arbiters settle disputes. ✅ **DONE** (the bond + liveness + prefer-bonded work).
- **Local custody** — your community's own guardians hold the mint (its own federation). ⬜ **the frontier.**

So "run your own fed" isn't a feature bolted on — it's the **completion of the sovereignty the bond already started.**
Local arbiters + local guardians = money that never leaves home. Frame the entire pull as *finishing what the bond began.*

## The real blocker isn't desire — it's difficulty
Communities don't skip their own fed because a shared fed is too comfortable. They skip it because **spinning up a
Fedimint federation is genuinely hard** (recruit guardians, run infra, a DKG ceremony). The pull only works if the
**path is walkable** — so the #1 lever is not motivation, it's **making it easy** (a guided wizard + a turnkey / hosted
guardian option, mirroring how the bond ceremony turned a scary on-chain thing into a guided flow). Everything below
assumes we lower that barrier; without it, no amount of aspiration moves anyone.

## The foundation you already built: guardians = your bonded arbiters
Your bonded arbiters are the members who **locked their own capital** to stand behind the community — the exact people
you'd trust to hold the keys. So the graduation path is natural and already seeded:
> A community with N funded bonded arbiters already has N vetted **guardian candidates.**

The bond/liveness layer isn't only for settling disputes — it's the **recruiting ground for a federation's guardians.**
That's the elegant bridge: **liveness** (arbiters show up + bond) → **sovereignty** (those arbiters become guardians).

## The pulls (positive — never friction)
1. **The visible ladder.** Give a community a tier it can *see* and *climb*: 🌍 **On the Global fed** (rides BLF/GBF) →
   ⚡ **Live** (bonded arbiters, trades flowing) → 🏛 **Sovereign** (runs its own fed). The liveness card already shows
   health; add the sovereignty rung so "someday, our own" is always in view — aspirational, not a nag.
2. **Control, made concrete.** *"On the Global fed, guardians you've never met hold your community's money. Your own
   fed → **your** guardians, chosen by your community."* Sovereignty you can actually name.
3. **Fees stay home** (the $ case). Fedimint guardians earn (gateway/mint fees). Show a thriving community the mirror:
   *"Your community moved ₿X last month — those fees went to the Global fed's guardians. Your own fed keeps them
   in-community."* A concrete economic reason for any community with real volume.
4. **Reputation is portable, so graduating costs nothing.** Identity + ratings live on **Nostr, not the fed.** When a
   community spins up its own mint, its whole reputation history **carries over** — only the ecash backing changes.
   Make migration a smooth "your community, now sovereign" moment, never a reset. Removing the *fear* of graduating is
   itself a pull.

## The movement mechanic (make it collective)
Owning a fed is a **community** decision, so make it one:
- A **pledge**: members signal "I'd back our own fed"; bonded arbiters signal "I'd be a guardian."
- When the thresholds line up (enough pledges + enough willing guardians + enough volume), Chama surfaces:
  *"Your community is ready to go sovereign"* → the spin-up flow.
- It becomes a milestone the community reaches *together* and celebrates — not a solo chore.

## Staged rollout (post-v5)
- **S0 (cheap, ~now):** one honest line on the community / liveness card — *"🌍 Rides the Global fed · its own someday"*
  — plants the seed with zero friction. Just the ladder's top rung, shown as aspiration.
- **S1:** the **economic mirror** — a community's volume + fees-flowing-out, making the "keep it home" case real.
- **S2:** the **guardian bridge** — bonded arbiters see "you could be a guardian of [community]'s fed"; a pledge / recruit surface.
- **S3 (the unlock):** the **turnkey fed spin-up** — a guided ceremony + a hosted-guardian option so the DKG isn't a
  wall. Hard, highest-leverage build; the whole ladder is aspiration-only until this exists.
- **S4:** smooth **migration** (reputation carry-over) + the 🏛 **Sovereign** badge + a celebration moment.

## The through-line
Don't make sharing annoying to force decentralization. Make **owning aspirational, walkable, and a natural completion
of the bond** — the local arbiters you already built, now local guardians. The bond didn't just secure trades; it grew
the seed of every community's own federation. This is the lever that actually moves Chama from a few mega-feds to a
world of local ones.
